📝 What is an ABN (Advance Beneficiary Notice)?

When Medicare is likely to deny a service as not medically necessary, have the patient sign an ABN (Form CMS-R-131) before the service. With a signed ABN, bill with the GA modifier — liability shifts to the patient. Without an ABN, use GZ and the practice absorbs the cost.

Golden rule: no ABN + denial = your loss. Check coverage first, get the signature, then bill.
