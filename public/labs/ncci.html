📖 How to read an NCCI indicator:
0 = NEVER together — no modifier overrides this. Drop one code.
1 = Together ONLY with a qualifying modifier (59, XE/XS/XP/XU) on the column-2 code.
9 = No edit exists (or trap: the codes are inherently bundled — delete, don't modify).
