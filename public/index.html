Curriculum

The complete RCM journey, stage by stage

Follow a claim from patient registration to final payment — the same path every US medical bill travels.

01

Registration

Capture clean patient demographics — 40% of denials start here.

02

Eligibility

Verify coverage, copay, deductible and prior-auth rules (270/271).

03

Medical Coding

ICD-10-CM, CPT and HCPCS — link every procedure to its diagnosis.

04

Charge Entry

Build the claim: CMS-1500/837P or UB-04/837I, done right.

05

Submission

Scrub, submit, and never confuse a rejection with a denial.

06

Payment Posting

Read the ERA/EOB, post allowed amounts, adjustments and patient share.

07

Denials

Decode CO/PR codes, appeal or correct, fix the root cause.

08

A/R Follow-up

Work aging buckets, beat the KPIs: <40 days in A/R, >95% clean claims.
