Curriculum

What you'll master, stage by stage

Follow a claim's full journey — the same path every US medical bill travels.

  1. Patient registration — capture clean demographics; ~40% of denials start with front-end errors.
  2. Eligibility verification — check active coverage, copay, deductible, coinsurance (270/271).
  3. Prior authorization — know when payers require approval before the visit.
  4. Medical coding — ICD-10-CM (why), CPT (what), HCPCS (supplies); link every code.
  5. Charge entry & claim creation — build CMS-1500/837P claims that survive scrubbing.
  6. Submission & tracking — clearinghouses, rejections vs denials, timely filing limits.
  7. Payment posting — read the ERA/EOB; post payer, adjustment and patient portions.
  8. Denial management & A/R — appeal or correct, fix root causes, work aging buckets.
